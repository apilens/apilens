---
title: "Ingest health"
description: "What the ingest health counters mean, and what to do when one of them moves."
---

**Project settings → Ingest health** shows, per app, what reached APILens and what was lost on the way. It has two parts:

- **At the ingest edge**: what the edge did with the records the app sent, per UTC day over the last 7 days, and what the app's SDKs reported dropping before sending (**Dropped by the SDK**). Read from `GET /api/v1/apps/{id}/ingest-stats`.
- **In the SDKs**: what the app's SDKs could not deliver, summed over the instances that sent a heartbeat in the last hour. Read from `sdk_stats` of `GET /api/v1/apps/{id}/status` (see [Heartbeat](/ingest/heartbeat)). These counters run since each process started.

## At the ingest edge

| Counter | Counts | When it moves |
|---|---|---|
| **Accepted** | Records stored (requests, logs, spans, deploys). | This is the app's traffic. A sudden drop to zero with the app still serving requests means the SDK stopped sending: check **In the SDKs** and the app's capture policy. |
| **Rejected** | Records the edge did not store, by reason: the project's ingest quota (HTTP 429), the app refused, `invalid` (a record with a bad timestamp, id or field; the rest of its batch is stored), or `storage_unavailable` (storage could not take the rows; the edge answered 503 and the SDK retries them, so they are usually stored later). | For the quota, lower the volume with a sample rate in **Project settings → Capture**, or raise it. `invalid` points at a host clock or an exporter's configuration. Steady `storage_unavailable` on a self-hosted install means ClickHouse is down or overloaded. |
| **Truncated** | Request fields (payloads, headers) cut to the server-side size cap. A record can count up to four times. | Expected for large bodies. If the cut part matters, capture less (headers or bodies off) or lower the body size cap in the SDK so it trims first. |
| **Masked** | Records in which the project's privacy policy masked at least one value. | Expected when the deny list matches. A jump after a release usually means a new field matches a pattern; check **Project settings → Privacy**. |

### Dropped by the SDK

Every SDK counts the records it drops before they reach the edge and reports the counts, per signal (requests, spans, logs) and reason, in its next [heartbeat](/ingest/heartbeat#drop-counters). The edge adds them up per app and hour. When any were reported, the panel shows a **Dropped by the SDK** tile next to **Rejected**, a table of the drops by reason and signal, and a column in the per-day table. Nothing extra is shown while no drops are reported.

Nothing an SDK loses is silent: an exception that never arrived shows up here as a `logs` drop with its reason. The reasons are those of the [SDK counters](#in-the-sdks) below.

The edge keeps these counters in Valkey. On a self-hosted install without Valkey the panel says that no counters are kept.

## In the SDKs

| Counter | Counts |
|---|---|
| **Sent** | Records delivered. |
| **Waiting in queues** | Records buffered in the SDK, not yet sent. |
| **Failed batches** | Batches that failed to send. A failed batch is retried, so it is not always lost. |
| **Dropped** | Records lost, by reason below. |

| Reason | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `queue_full` | The SDK queue hit its record limit (`max_queue_size`). | The app produces records faster than they are sent. Raise the queue size, lower the sample rate, or check the network path to ingest. |
| `queue_bytes` | The SDK queue hit its memory limit (`max_queue_bytes`). | Usually large bodies. Capture fewer bodies or set a body size cap. |
| `non_retryable` | Ingest refused a batch with a 4xx (for example a revoked API key or an unknown app). | Check the SDK's API key and app id. The SDK logs the refusal. |
| `retries_exhausted` | A batch still failed when the SDK's retry window ran out (`max_retry_seconds`, 5 minutes by default; network errors, 5xx, 429). | Check that the app can reach `ingest.apilens.ai` (or your ingest host), and the **Rejected** counter for quota refusals. |
| `shutdown` | Records still queued when the process exited and the shutdown flush ran out of time (5 s by default). | Give the shutdown flush more time (`shutdown_timeout`) and the orchestrator a longer grace period. See [Delivery › Shutdown](/sdk-reference/delivery#shutdown). |
| `invalid`, `app_rejected` (Java) | Records ingest refused inside an otherwise accepted batch. | The same records are counted under **Rejected** at the edge; read its reason. |

Records the SDK shortened to fit a batch (`truncated` in the SDK's drop counts) are still sent and are not counted as dropped.
